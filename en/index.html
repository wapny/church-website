Welcome to Lighthouse of Hope Church in Riga

About us

Lighthouse of Hope Church

— is a living Christian community that combines active social ministry with the growth of the Messianic movement. Our story began more than 10 years ago with practical help for people, and today these values remain our priority.

Our history

The ministry grew out of the work of the charitable foundation Kraukļu fonds (the “Ravens Foundation”), which helped low-income families and people in crisis. Alexander Vapne was responsible for the foundation’s work in one of Riga’s remote districts.

At that time the Lord placed the same revelation about founding a church on the hearts of several ministers — they heard this word at the same moment, without having agreed beforehand. In 2015 Alexander and Nelia Vapne were ordained to pastoral ministry, and the Lighthouse of Hope church was officially established.

Social ministry is our priority!

We believe that faith is strong in deeds. Our church actively supports those who need help and care:

  • Help for people struggling with various addictions.
  • Support for low-income families and people in difficult life circumstances.
  • Care and ministry for the elderly.
  • Support for people with disabilities.

The church also runs active and growing programs for children, teenagers and young people.

Messianic community “Shofar”

Pastor Alexander Vapne has Jewish roots. In 2020, after the passing of his father, Messianic pastor Leonid Vapne, Alexander took over the ministry, merging his father’s community with the Lighthouse of Hope church.

Today the Messianic movement is one of the church’s key priorities. In the Shofar community we honor biblical traditions, study the Word and keep Shabbat on Saturdays.
